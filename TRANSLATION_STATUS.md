# Translation status

SafeHands ships English, Nigerian Pidgin, Hindi, Yoruba, Hausa, Arabic, Bengali, French, Portuguese, Spanish, and Swahili strings.

- The non-English strings in this MVP are **machine-drafted and need native speaker review**.
- They are not clinically validated and must not replace local clinical guidance.
- Voice recognition and speech synthesis support depends on the device/browser. If a local voice is unavailable, SafeHands keeps the text path available and uses the closest available voice only when the device selects one.
- Before public health deployment, native speakers and qualified health/safety reviewers must approve every translated safety instruction.
