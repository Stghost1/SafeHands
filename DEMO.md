# SafeHands two-minute demo

This demo works with the built app in airplane mode after the service worker has installed once.

1. Start the app with `npm run dev`, open the printed local URL, and choose **English** (or a local language).
2. Open **Health check**. Tap **Trouble breathing** or choose the voice button and say “I cannot breathe.” Voice is optional; the chip and text box are always available.
3. Submit. Point out the **Emergency** text, the spoken/read-aloud control, the local emergency call buttons, short action steps, “what not to do”, and the clear non-diagnosis disclaimer.
4. Open **Settings**, turn on **Easy Mode**, **High contrast**, and the larger text size. Show that the result remains visual as well as spoken.
5. Open **Scam Shield**. Paste: `Urgent: approve this UPI collect request and enter your PIN to receive a refund.`
6. Submit. Point out the exact **SCAM** verdict, score, matched phrases, plain-language reasons, safe actions, India 1930 reporting guidance, and family warning share text.
7. Switch language from Settings, then open **Find care** to show offline seed/sample labels. The app displays an **Offline-ready** status after registration.
8. Open **Climate**. Select **Use sample heat alert** and point out the red heat state, apparent temperature, wet-bulb screening estimate, PM2.5, and rainfall watch.
9. Select **Start adaptive hydration**, then open **Extreme heat and heatstroke**. Advance one step and use **Read this step**. The protocol remains available without a network request.
10. Show the **Community SOS dispatch** text preview and explain that the call/SMS/WhatsApp controls are device hand-offs, not confirmed rescue dispatches.

The health, scam, manual climate, sample climate, protocol, hydration, and SOS flows are deterministic and local. Live climate refresh is optional and user-triggered. No account, analytics, remote AI call, or secret input is required.
